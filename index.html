About Me

Game Development student specialized in programming, with a strong focus on C++ and Unreal Engine.

I am particularly interested in gameplay systems, engine architecture, and performance optimization.
I also work on music composition and sound design, as I believe audio plays a critical role in player experience.

I enjoy understanding how systems work at a deep level, both technically and creatively.
I love exploring all the possible ways to implement a function in order to determine the best option.
